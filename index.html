From two twelve-year-olds to two billion visits

Abracadabra Studio was founded in 2017 by Simoon68 and Opplo — two friends who met at twelve and taught themselves to make games on Roblox.

Our first group release, SharkBite, grew into one of Roblox's most-played survival games and swam off the platform entirely: official SharkBite toys, including a Nerf blaster, have shipped to shelves at Walmart, Target, ASDA and Tesco.

Today we build from our studio at Princes Dock, Liverpool, alongside a growing community of UK developers — and we're just getting started.
