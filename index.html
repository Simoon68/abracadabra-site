From rival clans to two billion visits

Abracadabra Studio is the work of Simoon68 and Opplo, who met in 2010 during Roblox's clans and guilds era. Their clans became allies, and running them is where they learned to build, script and manage a team. Years later they published their first game together, Wings, in 2014 — followed in 2015 by their first hit, RoBowling. The Abracadabra group formed in 2017 with the release of SharkBite.

SharkBite grew into one of Roblox's most-played survival games without a single advert, and swam off the platform entirely: official SharkBite toys, including a Nerf blaster, have shipped to shelves at Walmart, Target, ASDA and Tesco.

Today we build from our studio at Princes Dock, Liverpool, alongside a growing community of UK developers — and we're just getting started.
